## Case-study evidence template — internal, not publishable copy

Record: redacted job reference/date; permission to publish; confirmed origin/destination at an appropriate level of detail; goods classification and quantities; customer problem; documented timing/handling constraints; actual arrangement; evidence of completed outcome; commercial/regulatory reviewer. Exclude sensitive security routes, identifiable source details and customer information without permission. Publish a factual case only after supporting records and customer permission are checked. Do not turn this template into a fictional example or testimonial.
