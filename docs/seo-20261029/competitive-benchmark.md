# Competitive benchmark — observed pages, not search rankings

Compared the live ADR Carriers `.com` homepage, core service pages, public technical files and separate `.co.uk` site with nine ADR Direct pages on 29 October 2026. `research-crawl.json` contains fetched titles, descriptions, headings and links. Competitor statements describe its marketing claims; their truth was not independently audited. Its text/images were not reused in ADR Carriers content.

| Search intent / journey | ADR Direct observation | ADR Carriers live observation | Development response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dangerous goods courier UK | Homepage title targets courier UK; specialist cards and phone/quote actions | ADR-led title and cautious short homepage, with core links | Dedicated transport proposition; courier intent consolidated into existing ADR service URL; stronger enquiry prompt and actions |
| Urgent transport | Separate same-day page, visible route from menu and homepage | Urgent/cross-border intent mostly combined in Europe page | Separate time-critical assessment guide; no imported same-day promise |
| Lithium | Dedicated lithium-battery-courier page; mentions UN3480/UN3481 and damaged/defective collection | Existing battery page covers broad classification and condition | Keep lithium hub; add distinct UN configuration and damaged/defective assessment guides with reciprocal links |
| Class 1 / Class 7 | Dedicated pages in primary navigation, repeated CTA journey and trust claims | Existing service pages linked from home | Retain established URLs; improve shipment-specific requirements, FAQ answers, related links and phone/quote access |
| UK–Europe / Ireland | Not prominent in the sampled primary navigation | Existing Europe/NI page and broad homepage enquiry coverage | European route guide plus a specific Ireland/NI guide explaining destination and crossing questions |
| Regional | Manchester page and several northern city/county footer links | Cumbria contact evidence, broad UK positioning | Publish only evidenced Cumbria enquiry page; defer unsupported locality claims |
| Credibility | Prominent DGSA/RPA/DfT/vehicle claims and a company-registration footer | Cautious live `.com` copy; older `.co.uk` makes broader claims | Add practical load-specific expertise and verified contact facts; do not borrow competitor credentials or fabricate reviews/cases |
| Conversion | Repeated phone/quote links; homepage explains enquiry-to-delivery steps | Quote form and phone available, shorter guidance journey | Phone in header; classification/route/timing checklist; local form failure/success tests and fallback contact |
| Architecture | Class menu, FAQs and specialist-to-quote journeys | Existing public service and sector hubs | Preserve existing URLs, add relevant service cross-links, clear breadcrumbs and one route per distinct intent |

The separate `.co.uk` site creates overlapping service content and mixed brand journeys. Its observed homepage HTML was approximately 1.97 MB, compared with approximately 26 KB for live `.com` HTML and 886 KB for the competitor homepage at collection time. These are raw HTML sizes, not complete page-transfer or performance scores. A smaller document does not prove better field performance or rankings.

No Search Console or reliable SERP tracking was available. Competitor ranking position, ADR Carriers rankings, impressions, clicks, search demand and likely traffic gains are all **unverified**. The opportunities here follow commercial intent and observed information architecture, not fabricated keyword metrics.
